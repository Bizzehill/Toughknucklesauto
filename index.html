About Us

Built on Hard Work
& Honest Hands

Tough Knuckles Automotive was founded by Cory and Ashley Adams right here in Holland, Ohio. What started as a passion for fixing cars the right way has grown into a trusted community repair shop — built on the belief that every customer deserves straight answers, quality parts, and work done right the first time.

Cory brings hands-on mechanical expertise to every vehicle that comes through our doors. Ashley keeps everything running smoothly behind the scenes. Together, they've built a shop that feels less like a business and more like a neighbor you can count on.

We're not a big chain. We don't have gimmicks or unnecessary upsells. We're your neighbors, and we treat your car like it's our own.
